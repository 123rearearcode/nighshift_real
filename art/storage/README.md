# Storage pictures

What each item looks like in the storage under the counter (0.57.3, ART-10), seen from above, one file per
quarter turn: `<name>_r0.png` to `<name>_r3.png` (r1 is a quarter turn clockwise of r0). The light stays at
the top-left in every turn. A picture is used only when it exactly fits the item's footprint in 44 px cells:

- Parts (`gpu`, `ram`, `cpu`, `cooler`, `psu`, `storage_hdd`, `storage_ssd`): illustrated, comic-book and
  semi-realistic (user, 23 November 2026), at full size, 44 px per cell.
- PCs (`pc_tower`, `pc_compact`) and tools (`vacuum`, `brush`, `pick`, `cloth`, `syringe`): pixel art at
  22 px per cell, shown at exactly 2x.

Items without a picture here (an NVMe drive, for example) keep the plain coloured box. Credits, approval
evidence and hashes: docs/ART_ASSET_LOG.md. New or changed pictures need the user's approval first.
